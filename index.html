Privacy Policy

Prototype policy · review before launch

Information this prototype uses

This prototype lets you enter an area manually or ask your browser for GPS coordinates. GPS access requires your permission. In this version, the location is displayed in the page and is not intentionally sent to a SignalScout India backend.

Speed test

The speed-test feature sends test requests to a third-party speed-test service. That service may receive technical information such as your IP address and request metadata. Its own privacy terms apply. Results may vary with your device, signal, browser, and server route.

Storage and sharing

When a user chooses a network and enters an area/locality name, city name, or PIN code, the speed-test result (download, upload, latency, jitter, selected network, entered area label and timestamp) is sent to the SignalScout India Supabase database to support local comparisons. The comparison page returns aggregated averages and test counts by operator for matching area labels; it does not expose raw individual test rows. Do not enter passwords, financial details, or other sensitive information. Results can be approximate and depend on device, signal and test-server route.

Future features

Before adding community reports, saved results, analytics, or a backend, this policy must be updated to explain what is collected, why it is collected, how long it is kept, who receives it, and how users can request deletion.
